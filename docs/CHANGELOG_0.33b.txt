==============================================================
            WAR OF SOULS 2 - PATCH 0.33b CHANGELOG
==============================================================


--------------------------------------------------------------
 I. GENERAL BALANCE AND MECHANICS
--------------------------------------------------------------

 1. AgiMoveBonus: 1.0 -> 0.6.
    Agility now gives less movement speed.

 2. All heroes +25 attack range.
    Melee 100 -> 125; Raiden / Alter / Crocodile 120 -> 145;
    Alucard / Ainz / Frieren 700 -> 725.

 3. Removed passive damage reduction by hero level
    (was -5% at lvl 12, -10% at lvl 24, -15% at lvl 35).

 4. Agility gives 20% more physical resistance than regular armor
    (0.12 per Agi instead of 0.1).

 5. Intelligence now gives magic resistance
    (armor formula, 0.12 per Int).

 6. Healing reductions now also reduce HP regeneration
    (Dark Holy Grail, Inori T, Hell Blaze, Kirito T, Rimuru Q, etc).

 7. Added healing and mana restoration amplification
    (used by Shun Shun Rikka and Frozen Heart).

 8. Dead heroes no longer gain HP from healing or lifesteal.

 9. When your hero takes damage, selection returns to the hero
    (max once per 2 sec).

10. Damage numbers are merged per player and damage type
    over 0.1 sec - less clutter on screen.


--------------------------------------------------------------
 II. ARENA AND ROUNDS
--------------------------------------------------------------

 1. Decoration explosions scale with the round number:
    were a flat 300 / 200 / 500,
    now 100 / 100 / 200 + 25 per round.

 2. Train damage:
    was 250 + 100 x round / 500 + 125 x round,
    now 200 / 400 + 50 per round.

 3. Sukuna: the arena shatter deals % of max HP per tick,
    from 1% up to 15%, ramping up after 7 sec.

 4. Sukuna spawns at 1 of 5 random points (center or 4 points
    3000 away from it). A minimap ping warns you beforehand.
    New hint: "move closer to Sukuna".

 5. Sometimes "Thomas" arrives instead of the train.

 6. Test mode: the round starts instantly once everyone stands
    in the start zone.

 7. Captain pick time: 10 -> 15 sec.

 8. The first window after the pick is 30 sec longer (once).


--------------------------------------------------------------
 III. NEW SYSTEMS AND COMMANDS
--------------------------------------------------------------

 1. Votes:
    - "-ff" starts a team surrender vote. All teammates must
      agree. Retry after 30 sec. No answer counts as "no".
    - Kick vote requires all other players to agree.
    - The host can disable FF.

 2. Team pings: hold Z, choose an order, click the map.
    Shown in the world and on the minimap. 2.5 sec cooldown.
    Incoming pings can be muted.

 3. Damage Stats: damage dealt and taken by source for the
    round, the game and the previous round.
    F3 resets the game counter, F4 restores it.

 4. Range preview: hovering an ability or item shows its
    range / area circle.

 5. Career and ranks:
    - Rank ladder from NOOB to SOUL KING.
    - A match counts only with more than 4 players.
    - Saves are written in parts; profile import from WOS App.
    - New commands: "-careerinfo", "-saveinfo".

 6. "-killme" shows progress and is cancelled if the hero
    returns to base (1 sec in Test mode).

 7. "-help" opens a guide window for new players.

 8. Ability hotkeys can be rebound right on the buttons
    (key badges). Added a hotkey reset button.

 9. Hero guide with tabs for forms and ability kits.

10. Heroes appear on the minimap as portrait icons.
    Kirito clones look like the hero.


--------------------------------------------------------------
 IV. INTERFACE
--------------------------------------------------------------

 1. Brand new HUD: console, HP/MP bars, hero panel, buff dock
    with timers, shared meters (stacks, charges, bankai, etc).

 2. Ally ability cooldowns are visible; enemy ability tooltips
    can be read.

 3. Reworked: pick and Captain Draft screens, top scoreboard,
    toolbar, minimap, ESC menu, emotes (now with pages).

 4. Shop:
    - Tabs: Base / Strength / Agility / Intelligence /
      Universal / Attack.
    - Quick Buy: recipe-based auto-buy queue that saves
      your build.


--------------------------------------------------------------
 V. ITEMS - NEW
--------------------------------------------------------------

 1. KUSANAGI (Katana + Pakkun +12), 5000 gold
    +40 ATK, +15 all stats.
    Active: dashes to every enemy within 700 range of the
    target point, hitting each for 2x ATK. Cooldown 20 sec.

 2. KUSANAGI: BLAZE RELEASE / WATER RELEASE / CHIDORI KUSANAGI
    (Kusanagi + Piro / Hydro / Electro Slime)
    +60 ATK, +20 all stats.
    Strikes deal 3x ATK and apply the element's debuff.
    Only one elemental Kusanagi can be carried.
    All Kusanagi share one cooldown.

 3. HASHIRAMA CELLS (Avalon + Tome of Strength +10)
    +15 Str, +10 all stats, +10 HP regen.
    Passive: allied heroes within 700 heal for 15% of the
    damage you take.
    Active: sacrifice 10% current HP to gain +15 Str for 10 sec.

 4. SHUN SHUN RIKKA (Necklace +500 + Pakkun +15)
    +15 all stats, +500 HP.
    Aura (1200): +18% healing and HP regen; stuns, silences
    and roots last 0.5 sec shorter.

 5. ASHISOGI JIZO (Tome of Agility +10 + Pakkun +10)
    +10 Agi, +10 all stats.
    Physical ability damage of 100+ poisons the target:
    0.4x Agi per sec for 5 sec.

 6. SCROLL OF MASTERY - 5000 gold, available from round 15.
    Permanently grants +2 all stats. Takes no inventory slot.


--------------------------------------------------------------
 V. ITEMS - CHANGES
--------------------------------------------------------------

 7. Rhitta: +30 Str -> +10 Str and +10 all stats.
    Each enemy hit grants +5 Str for 10 sec (up to +35).

 8. Frozen Heart: new aura (1200) - +20% mana regen and
    mana restoration.

 9. Philosopher's Stone: was 35% heal from hits of 600+
    (cooldown 3 sec). Now heals 15% of all damage dealt to
    enemies, no threshold, no cooldown.

10. Hell Blaze:
    - Duration 15 -> 10 sec.
    - Reduces both healing and HP regen.
    - At 7 stacks burns 1.5% max mana per sec
      (was 10 mana per sec).

11. Murasame: bleed 0.45x ATK per sec for 5 sec
    (was 50 + 0.6x ATK). Each new attack adds its damage to the
    next tick and shortens the bleed by 1 sec.

12. Sakura Sesshou: places a totem that reveals invisible units
    in a 3000 area. Every 2 sec lightning hits a random enemy
    within 1100 (invisible first) for 1.25x main stat.

13. Sacred Gear Booster: using an active item cuts that item's
    cooldown by 5 sec.

14. Death Note: threshold 800 -> 600, now also works with
    physical damage.

15. Demon-Dweller Sword: bonus 30% -> 33%, threshold 500 -> 250.

16. Mera Mera no Mi: 25 + 1% HP (max 300) -> 40 + 1.15% HP
    (max 250).

17. Hokage Hat Evolved: root 2.5 -> 3 sec, radius 550 -> 600.

18. Susanoo Shield: cooldown 10 -> 8 sec.

19. Ryujin Jakka / Nejibana / Gonryomaru: cooldown 7 -> 6 sec.

20. Nichirin Sword Whip / True Zangetsu: resistance reduction
    14% -> 16%.

21. Okarun Egg: cooldown reduction 12.5% -> 14%.

22. Prison Realm: cooldown reduction 25% -> 22.5%.

23. Cup of Tea: default threshold 1000 -> 500.

24. Tachikaze: cooldown 1 -> 0.95 sec.

25. Fixed item names: Necklace (was Neklace), Raijin's Wrath.

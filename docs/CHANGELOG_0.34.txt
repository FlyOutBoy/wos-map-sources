War of Souls 2 - v0.34 (changes since 0.33e)

NEW HERO: FROSTNOVA
Q Ice Javelins: 2/2/4/4/6 charges, recharge 20/19/18/17/16 sec, stops at the first enemy.
  Winter's Scar: pierces enemies, +30% projectile speed.
W Northern Winds: widening wave, knockback 450 that fades with distance (x0.35 at max range).
  Winter's Scar: 3 waves, knockback 750, each enemy is hit once, enemies inside Icefield are not pushed out.
E Frost Nova: frost burst and a 10 sec freezing field (INT x 0.35-0.75 per sec).
  Inside the field Frostnova takes 30% less damage, enemies receive 35% less healing.
  Winter's Scar: 1 sec stun when the cast ends, frost aura around her (INT x 0.75 per sec).
R Icefield: 4-8 sec blizzard, INT x 1 per sec, +INT x 0.05 per sec for each Yeti Princess stack.
  Winter's Scar: 2 sec root on cast, 2 sec root on the first attempt to leave, random bursts every 0.36 sec (INT x 0.5, 450 radius).
T Winter's Scar: 20 sec, refills Q, W/E/R cooldowns -10 sec, Song Shield blocks one hit for 4 sec and is renewed by each song verse.
F Yeti Princess: stacking slow, up to 5 stacks (5% for 4 sec each).
  Winter's Scar: 8% for 6 sec, INT x 0.15 damage per stack per sec, dispellable.

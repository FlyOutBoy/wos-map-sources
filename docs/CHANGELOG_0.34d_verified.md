**War of Souls 2 — 0.34d** ⚔️

**Heroes**

- All heroes: base HP **250 → 450**.
- **Inori:** melee → ranged attacks, with crystal shots during E and T2. At level 25+, choosing T1 unlocks automatic E rank 6 with 10% physical and magic resistance. During T2, the allied aura and Instability passive are disabled; enemies within 1400 take 10% more physical and magic damage.
- **Okarun:** F speed gain **5% → 4.25% of maximum speed per second**. R no longer grants invulnerability during the dash; the combo still does.
- **Frostnova:** movement speed **290 → 275**; Q mana **70 at every rank → 70/75/80/85/90**. Q INT scaling **1/1.5/2/2.5/3 → 1/1.35/1.7/2.05/2.4**; its speed bonus during T **30% → 15%**. W projectile speed **1430 → 1540**. T shield **4 → 2 sec**; F damage per stack **0.15 → 0.12 × INT**, and its level 35 T bonus **0.25 → 0.12 × INT**.
- **Ainz:** F → Q mine ring **6 → 5 mines**, lifetime **40 → 30 sec**. Q/Q2 damage reduced by **0.2 × INT at every rank**.
- **Bazz B:** damaging abilities apply Fire from level **35 → 12**.
- **Alter Saber:** R transformation **0.75 → 0.25 sec**. R2 field **1.5 → 3.5 sec**, root **1.5 → 2.5 sec**.
- **Crocodile:** Q + W combo stun **1.5 → 0.7 sec before level 25**, with a weaker pull. Level 25 restores the full combo. T2 retains T's damage resistance until detonation.
- **Mahoraga:** R duration **1 → 2 sec**, healing every 0.5 sec with the same total heal.
- **Kirito:** R follow-up strikes now count as basic attacks, with two quick hits or four strong hits.
- **Patriot:** fixed stance transitions, repeated movement bonuses and ultimate cooldown transfer.
- **Akainu:** fixed magma hand effects remaining after death or interrupted abilities.
- **Gojo:** reduced the number of T visual effects.

**37 new items and upgrades** ✨

- **13 new elemental actives:** each stat now has Fire, Water and Lightning options for a single target and an area. Crafted from an elemental zanpakuto and a +20 stat tome; cost **7500 gold**, independent cooldowns. Their attacks use travelling projectiles, delayed blasts, whirlpools, waves and lightning storms.
- STR: **Emberbreaker, Leviathan's Grasp, Leviathan's Anchor, Thunder Verdict, Thunder Bastion**.
- AGI: **Cinder Fang, Ashen Crossfire, Mirror Tide, Volt Thread**.
- INT: **Phoenix Seal, Crimson Scripture, Siren's Tear, Constellation Coil**.
- **Zanka no Tachi:** Fire lasts 7 sec, starting at 70 damage/sec; each reapplication adds 40/sec, up to 300.
- **Kyoka Suigetsu:** converts Fire/Water into Steam, adds damage on reapplication and increases your physical damage against the target.
- **Koko Gonryo Rikyu:** Lightning deals additional damage every second.
- **Tobiume / Suzumebachi:** strengthen Overload / Electrized respectively.
- **Blood Pact Ring:** exchange 400 HP for 400 mana, 12 sec cooldown. **Evolved:** keeps mana at a minimum of 25% for 5 sec; Five Elements Seal overrides it.
- **Armament Haki / Evolved:** blocks the next debuff; Evolved also grants 3 sec of debuff immunity.
- **Naofumi Shield Evolved:** blocks 40 damage per hit, rising to 100 during its active.
- **Sukuna's Finger:** prevents healing and HP regeneration for 7 sec and slows by 15%; cannot be dispelled.
- **Five Elements Seal:** prevents mana recovery for 7 sec; casting or removing its secondary seal causes 2 sec of silence.
- **Rokushiki: Tekkai:** blocks basic attack damage for 4 sec.
- **Samehada:** attacks restore 4% of your maximum mana and drain 4% of the target's maximum mana.
- **Will of D.:** lethal damage leaves you at 1 HP for 3 sec; cooldown resets every round.
- **Kairoseki Cuffs:** removes positive buffs and reduces the target's damage by 18% for 4 sec.
- **Hyorinmaru Ice Coffin:** 2 sec of frozen invulnerability, usable while stunned. Its own hotkey can be changed with CHANGE or `-coffin <key>` and saved with Save Keys.
- **Return by Death:** returns you to a marked position after 4 sec; removing its buff cancels the return.
- Added Evolved versions of **Fairy Tail Emblem, Ryujin Jakka, Nejibana, Gonryomaru, Mirror of Yata and Philosopher's Stone**.

- **Okarun Egg / Prison Realm:** price **7500 → 5500**, main stat **30 → 25**, HP regeneration **20 → 15**.
- **Philosopher's Stone:** price **6000 → 5000**, healing **15% → 18% of damage dealt**. Evolved can double that healing to 36% for 3 sec.
- **Mirror of Yata:** price **7500 → 6000**, reflection **35% → 30%**. Evolved reflects 75% during its 4 sec active and has no reflection range limit.
- **Witch Sin:** price **5000 → 6000**, triggered magic resistance **18% → 21%**. **Emperor's Blue Armor:** price **5000 → 6000**, triggered physical reduction **20% → 23%**. **Jewel of Four Souls:** price **4500 → 6000**, heal **500 → 500 + 3% maximum HP**.
- **Raijin's Wrath, Shark Trail, Red Cup, Red Flower, Rhitta:** price **5000 → 7500**, all stats **10 → 15**, new recipes and icons. Their +10 main stat bonus remains.
- **Sacred Gear Booster:** damage bonus **3 → 2.5 × main stat**.
- **Naofumi Shield:** cooldown **24 → 27 sec**. **Lust Sin:** **5 → 7 sec**. **Wrath Sin:** **20 → 23 sec**. **Shiki Knife Evolved:** active cooldown **8 → 9 sec**.
- **Susanoo:** mana cost **125 → 105**. **Quincy Cross:** bonus damage **40 → 45**.
- **Black Heart / Sword of Mihawk:** stat gain per stack **6 → 7**.
- **Black Clover:** each resistance stack now reduces damage taken by 1%.
- **Incursio:** added 5 HP/sec and 2.5 mana/sec regeneration.
- **Hashirama Cells:** active Strength bonus **15 → 20**.
- **Chogurt Evolved:** added an allied aura restoring 8 mana/sec within 1500.
- Active item cast ranges above 1600 reduced to 1600; **Shiki Knife Evolved: 2300 → 2100**, **Kusanagi: 900 → 1425**. Elemental Kusanagi can be exchanged for another element in the shop for free.
- **Bunshin Scroll and Turbo Neko** can no longer be carried together.

**Fixes and interface** 🐛

- Casting a movement ability while rooted no longer wastes its mana or cooldown.
- Fixed Jewel of Four Souls healing an enemy instead of its owner.
- Fixed item damage, reflection, elemental damage and poison causing repeated item activations. Non-Fire debuffs can be refreshed by item damage once per application; abilities can keep refreshing them. Fire remains unrestricted.
- Evolved elemental zanpakuto apply their element on qualifying hits even while their explosion is on cooldown. Elemental debuffs also work on clones.
- Lust Sin now pierces the Laxus T / Mahoraga ultimate shields. Mirror of Yata no longer reflects damage into an active Naofumi Shield.
- Samehada works with Starrk E and Raiden's magic attacks. Fixed lingering Lightning effects and several Evolved item activation/upgrade issues.
- Silence is shown on the portrait and over ability buttons.
- Shift + right-click on Quick Buy or a recipe tells allies what you plan to buy.
- AUTOBUY is green when enabled and red when disabled. Universal items have a second shop page.
- Item range previews distinguish cast range from the full area of effect.
- Periodic recovery no longer floods combat with green numbers; regeneration is shown on the HP/mana bars.
- Reduced interface redraws and Fire effect particles; adjusted inventory spacing and ally color icons.

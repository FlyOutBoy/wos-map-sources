# ⚔️ WAR OF SOULS 2 — PATCH 0.33c

## 🖥️ Interface
- Hero portraits sit **5%** higher in the portrait frame (no longer touching its bottom edge).
- Fixed full-price item refunds after moving inventory slots or buying again; each eligible purchase keeps its own **15-second** refund window.
- Added a top-left warning when ban choices lock with **3 seconds remaining**; conflicting selections explain why a ban was rejected.
- Restored the **animated hero portrait** with **spell targeting**, enlarged by **2.5%**. Camera and voice clicks now use the level plate.
- Fixed portrait position and size drifting; moved the short camera/voice hint to the level plate.
- Dead heroes now show an **empty HP bar** in the top panel.
- Collapsing **Common Info** moves gold and next-round income into the inventory header.
- Centered the four buttons above the portrait.
- Added optional **auto-select** for your living hero every **0.25s**.
- Fixed missing range circles for long-range abilities.
- Fixed normal and fast item sales from the currently controlled enemy or allied test hero.

## 🛠️ Shop Fixes
- Naofumi Shield, Turbo Neko, Bunshin Scroll, Cup of Tea and Cup of Tea Evolved moved from Universal to Defensive (own row).
- **Quick Buy:** builds intermediate recipes first (the more valuable one first), then upgrade parts, then the final item — e.g. Tsuchikage Hat before Pakkun +8 for the Evolved hat.
- Fixed a desync when a shop was open at round end with a never-priced item in the inventory.
- Scoreboard (F2) no longer reads heroes and items on one client only (possible desync when opened at round end).
- **Scroll of Mastery:** fixed purchases from round **15**, including with a full inventory. Grants **+2 to all stats**.
- Fixed a **paused-game gold exploit** involving repeated sales and purchases.

## 🏅 Team Awards
- **MVP** now comes from the winning team; **SVP** comes from the losing team. Neither is assigned at a tied score.

- Frostnova: added compact duration icons for Icefield and Frost Nova fields/aura; Winter's Scar and its shield now retain duration while paused. Enhanced Icefield catches blinks farther beyond its edge.
- Fixed the auto-select toggle in MAIN sandbox mode.

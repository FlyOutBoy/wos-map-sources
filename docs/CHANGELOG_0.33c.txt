# ⚔️ WAR OF SOULS 2 — PATCH 0.33c

## 🖥️ Interface
- Dead heroes now show an **empty HP bar** in the top panel.
- Collapsing **Common Info** moves gold and next-round income into the inventory header.
- Centered the four buttons above the portrait.
- Added optional **auto-select** for your living hero every **0.25s**.
- Fixed missing range circles for long-range abilities.
